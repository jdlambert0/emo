### ✅ ADDED ADVANCED MHH CONCEPTS

#### 1. **Dual Classification of People** ⭐
People exist on TWO states simultaneously:
- ON {self} map (as attachment)
- OFF {self} map (as external person)

**Why it matters**: Explains complex mixed emotions from single event.

**Example**: Your child causes $15,000 damage →
- Disappointed (child on map → sadness)
- Angry (child as external person → anger at external attack)
- Embarrassed (reflection on {self} → embarrassment)

**All 3 emotions from ONE event!**

---

#### 2. **Percentage Split Perceptions** ⭐
Person-caused Perceptions split into percentages:

**Partner says something hurtful:**
- 60% from "partner on {self} map" → SADNESS (hurt)
- 40% from "partner as external person" → ANGER

**Result**: "I'm hurt AND angry" (mathematically explained)

**Guidelines**:
- Life partner: 70% on map / 30% external
- Close family: 60% on map / 40% external
- Good friend: 50% on map / 50% external
- Acquaintance: 30% on map / 70% external
- Stranger: 5% on map / 95% external

---

#### 3. **Embedded {self} Maps** ⭐
People on your {self} map get their OWN {self} map embedded.

**Example**: You → Partner → Partner's Child → Child's Soccer Team

**Why it matters**: Explains empathy chains (you feel sad about partner's child's game loss through recursive {self} maps)
