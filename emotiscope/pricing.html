Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is my data really private?

Absolutely. Everything is stored locally on your device. We never see your conversations, emotions, or API keys. You're in complete control.

💳 Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! There are no contracts or commitments. Cancel anytime from your account settings, and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

🔑 Do I need to provide my own API key?

For all tiers, you bring your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. This keeps your costs transparent and your data private. Keys are stored locally on your device.

💰 Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, just email us for a full refund—no questions asked.

👥 Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

🏢 Do you offer team or enterprise plans?

Yes! For teams of 5+ or enterprise deployments, contact us for custom pricing and features including SSO, compliance support, and dedicated onboarding.
