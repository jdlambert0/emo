## Understanding the Webb Equation

**EP ∆ P = ER**

- **EP** = What you expected/wanted
- **P** = What actually happened (your perception)
- **ER** = Your emotional reaction

The GPT will break down every conflict using this framework.

---

## Key Concepts

### {self} Map
Everything that defines who you are:
- People (partner, family, friends)
- Values (respect, autonomy, fairness)
- Accomplishments (career, skills)
- Life story (identity, reputation)

Power levels: 1-10 (higher = stronger emotional reaction)

### Dual Classification
People exist in TWO states:
1. ON your {self} map (as part of your identity)
2. OFF your {self} map (as separate person)

This explains mixed emotions like "disappointed AND angry at my son"

### Percentage Splits
Person-caused hurts split:
- 60% from them on your {self} map → SADNESS (hurt)
- 40% from them as external person → ANGER

This is why you're "hurt AND angry" - it's mathematically predictable!
