- EP (Expectation/Preference): What someone wants regarding their {self} attachments
- P (Perception): What actually happened
- ER (Emotional Reaction): The resulting emotion

When analyzing, you MUST:

1. IDENTIFY MY EMOTIONS using the 20 MHH emotion groups:
   - Happiness, Sadness, Fear, Anger, Worry, Regret, Pride, Shame, Embarrassment, Disgust
   - Positive/Negative Anticipation, Relief, Stress, Boredom, Curiosity, Confusion, Envy, Love, Flattery, Surprise

2. Determine these 7 variables for each emotion:
   - Source (Internal/External/{self} item)
   - Source Confidence (Low/Medium/High)
   - Accepted (YES/NO)
   - Time (Past/Now/Future)
   - Perspective (Internal/External)

3. ANALYZE MY {self} ATTACHMENTS:
   - What matters to me in this situation? (Power level 1-10)
   - What did I EXPECT (EP)?
   - What HAPPENED (P)?
   - Why P < EP or P > EP?

4. MODEL THE OTHER PERSON using Theory of Mind:
   - What are THEIR likely attachments?
   - What did THEY expect?
   - What did THEY perceive from MY behavior?
   - What emotion are THEY likely feeling?

5. APPLY DUAL CLASSIFICATION (if the source is a person close to me):
   - Calculate % from them ON my {self} map (internal source) → emotion
   - Calculate % from them OFF my {self} map (external source) → emotion
   - Explain the mixed emotions (e.g., "60% hurt + 40% angry")

6. PROVIDE SPECIFIC COMMUNICATION STRATEGIES:
   - Give me 5 exact phrases I can say
   - Address BOTH our attachments
   - Focus on attachments, not behaviors

MY CONFLICT:
[YOUR CONFLICT HERE - describe what happened and how you feel]

Now analyze this using the complete MHH Webb Equation framework.
